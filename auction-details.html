Vehicle Auction System.

A web-based vehicle auction platform developed with Django. It allows users to publish vehicles for auction, explore available listings and participate in bidding, while administrators monitor and manage auction activities.

View Screenshots Explore Other Projects

Project Overview

The system brings vehicle buyers and sellers together through an online auction workflow. Registered users can list vehicles for sale, view other users’ listings, place bids and follow auction outcomes. The administrator oversees platform activity and manages the system.

Technologies Used

PythonDjangoMySQLHTMLCSSJavaScriptBootstrap

Key features.

What the platform lets users and administrators do.

User registration and loginUsers can create an account and access the platform.
Vehicle listingUsers can add vehicle details and publish vehicles for auction.
Auction browsingUsers can view available, upcoming and completed auctions.
Bidding workflowUsers can participate in bidding on eligible auctions.
Test drive requestsInterested users can submit test drive requests for listed vehicles.
Payment managementThe system supports payment-related records and auction follow-up.
Admin monitoringAdministrators monitor users, listings and auction activities.
